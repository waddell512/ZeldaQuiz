Ocarina of TimeThe Sage Trials

by the Skyward Prince and Butterbean

The Triforce speaks to you once, and what it says is that Senok is awake. An age ago the Fairy Prince of the Kokiri Forest put him under the roots of that wood and spent the whole of his own light doing it, and this season the last of that light ran out. Where Senok goes, light stops holding — lamps gutter and fairies go dark and fall out of the air — and he draws the life up out of the ground itself as he walks, so that the grass goes over grey behind him and nothing will take root in it for a year. No sword ends him. Only a sage’s light will put him back, and a sage is not appointed — a sage is made, by helping every part of Hyrule that needs you before he reaches it. Twelve places stand between you and the thirteenth, and the thirteenth is Senok. Choose who walks them with you, and answer as yourself; the element you carry will be plain long before the end.
