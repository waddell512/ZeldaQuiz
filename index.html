Ocarina of TimeThe Sage Trials

by the Skyward Prince

The Triforce speaks to you once, and what it says is that Vaskaal is coming: grey, faceless, twice the height of a man, and where it walks the colour goes out of the ground and nothing grows for a season. It says you are not ready. Twelve trials stand between you and the thirteenth, and the thirteenth is Vaskaal itself. Choose who walks them with you, and answer as yourself; the element you carry will be plain long before the end.

Before the first trial

Who goes with you?

One of them walks all thirteen at your shoulder. Nothing about this choice is scored — it only decides who is standing there when the wood makes you wait.
